OUR JOURNEY

Our Cute Story

From Arranged Marriage
to a Love Story

Once upon a time, there were two individuals living in two completely different worlds.

Neither of them was looking for love. Neither had any great expectations. And neither imagined that a simple “yes” to an arranged marriage would change their entire lives.

CHAPTERS OF US

Our Love Story

01
CHAPTER ONE

Two Separate Worlds

He had his own dreams, his own routines, and his own little world.

She had hers.

Two strangers. Two different personalities. Two different lives.

And then, one day, their families brought them together with one simple question:

“What do you think about getting married?”

They didn’t know each other. They didn’t have a love story. They didn’t even have a beginning.

Just a photograph, a name, and a meeting waiting to happen.

02
CHAPTER TWO

The Instagram Request

It was a Sunday morning.

Just another ordinary morning… until a little Instagram notification appeared.

“Someone requested to follow you.”

It was him.

She accepted the request, not thinking too much of it.

After all, it was just Instagram.

About an hour later…

“Hi.”

One simple message.

Nothing dramatic. Nothing romantic. Just a “Hi” from a complete stranger.

And somehow, that little “Hi” became the beginning of everything.

They started talking. Slowly at first.

Then, a week later, came another message…

“Can we meet?”
03
CHAPTER THREE

The First Meeting

And then came the day.

The day two complete strangers finally met.

There were awkward smiles. Shy glances. A little nervousness.

And probably a thousand thoughts running through their minds.

“What do I say?”
“What if we don’t get along?”
“Is this really happening?”

They sat together and started talking. At first, the conversation was a little formal.

There were questions about life, family, dreams and the future.

But somewhere between the questions… there was laughter.

And somewhere between the laughter… the awkwardness slowly disappeared.

It wasn’t love at first sight. There were no fireworks. No dramatic movie moment.

Just two people sitting together, slowly discovering each other.

But when the meeting ended… something felt different.

Maybe this was the beginning.

04
CHAPTER FOUR

From Strangers to Friends

They started talking. Then talking a little more.

Somehow, every conversation became longer than the last.

They discovered the little things about each other — the things they liked, the things that annoyed them, the silly habits, the dreams and the fears.

They began sharing jokes. Then secrets. Then their everyday lives.

And without even realising it, the person who was once a complete stranger became the first person they wanted to tell everything to.

05
CHAPTER FIVE

Maybe This Wasn’t Just Arranged

What started as an arranged marriage slowly became something neither of them had expected.

They weren’t just getting to know their future husband or wife.

They were falling in love with their best friend.

There were silly arguments. Endless laughs. Moments of:

“Why are you like this?”

And moments of:

“I can’t imagine my life without you.”

Love didn’t arrive all at once. It quietly grew…

conversation by conversation,
laugh by laugh,
day by day.

06
CHAPTER SIX

The “Yes”

And then came the question that had started it all.

“Are you both ready?”

This time, the answer wasn’t because their families suggested it.

It wasn’t because it was an arranged marriage.

It was because somewhere along the way…

They had chosen each other.

Two strangers who had once met because their families arranged it… were now choosing to spend the rest of their lives together.

07
CHAPTER SEVEN

Our Forever Begins

And that’s the funny thing about their story.

They didn’t start with a love story. They started with an arranged marriage.

But somewhere between the Instagram request, the first “Hi,” the first meeting, and the countless little moments that followed…

the arrangement became a friendship.

The friendship became a partnership.

And the partnership became love.

Because sometimes, the greatest love stories aren’t the ones that begin with “I love you.”

Sometimes they begin with:

“Someone requested to follow you.”

Then…

“Hi.”

And eventually…

“Can we meet?”

And somehow…

Two strangers became two souls who couldn’t imagine their story any other way.

And this is our story…

An arranged beginning.
An unexpected friendship.
A beautiful love.
And a forever we chose together.

And this is only the beginning…
